Austria - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-14.

Result: essentially nothing is published. The Interior Ministry (BMI) does issue
decrees and circulars to the settlement authorities (Niederlassungsbehörden of the
Länder), the police and the BFA, but it does not publish them.

Sources checked
- RIS, "Erlässe der Bundesministerien" (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Erlaesse/, queried
  through https://data.bka.gv.at/ris/api/v2.6/Sonstige?Applikation=Erlaesse):
  no document of the BMI at all. Full-text searches for Niederlassungs- und
  Aufenthaltsgesetz, NAG, Fremdenpolizeigesetz, FPG, Fremdengesetz, Fremdenrecht,
  Fremdenpolizei, Aufenthaltstitel, Aufenthaltsrecht, Aufenthaltsberechtigung,
  Aufenthaltsverbot, Niederlassung, Asyl, Asylgesetz, Asylwerber, Schubhaft,
  Abschiebung, Drittstaatsangehörige, Rot-Weiß-Rot, Ausländerbeschäftigungsgesetz,
  Ausländer, Einreise, Visum, Staatsbürgerschaft return only decrees of other
  ministries (mostly Justice) that mention these terms in passing.
  The two that actually deal with aliens-law procedure are saved in related-bmj/:
  Justice Ministry decrees telling courts, prosecutors and prisons what to report to
  the aliens authorities (2002, 2017). They are NOT instructions to immigration
  offices.
- BMI website: https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/ (Niederlassung und Aufenthalt, incl.
  sub-pages), /301/ (Asylwesen), /202/fremdenpolizei_und_grenzkontrolle/,
  /110/ (Verlautbarungen), /downloads/. Only legal texts, forms, statistics,
  brochures, and a specimen catalogue of residence titles ("Muster der
  Aufenthaltstitel und Dokumentationen", editions 2006-2026, under
  https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/start.aspx) - a reference aid, not downloaded.
  No Erlass, Durchführungserlass, "Handbuch zum NAG" or "Informationen für Behörden".
- BFA (https://www.bfa.gv.at/, incl. /402/ Broschüren): public brochures and
  country-of-origin reports only; no internal instructions.
- AMS (https://www.ams.at/organisation/ueber-ams/ams-richtlinien): publishes its
  federal guidelines, but none on the Ausländerbeschäftigungsgesetz / Rot-Weiß-Rot
  card procedure.
- Vienna MA 35 (https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/einwanderung-aufenthalt):
  applicant information only.
- Web searches for "Handbuch zum NAG", "Durchführungserlass NAG", BMI
  "Rundschreiben" on official (gv.at) sites: nothing published.

Where BMI instructions do surface
- Quoted in answers to parliamentary questions, e.g. the BMI circular of 30 March
  2021 to the settlement authorities on § 20(4) NAG and absences during COVID-19,
  described in https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVII/AB/6410/imfname_986635.pdf
  (not downloaded: a parliamentary answer, not the circular itself).
- Possibly through freedom-of-information portals (e.g. fragdenstaat.at); not
  checked, since that is not official publication.
