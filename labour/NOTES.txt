Austria - labour authority: Arbeitsmarktservice (AMS)
Collected 15 November 2026. Script: download_at_labour.py (python3 download_at_labour.py).

Role
The AMS decides employment permits (Beschäftigungsbewilligung) under the Ausländerbeschäftigungsgesetz (AuslBG)
and gives the binding labour-market opinion for the Red-White-Red Card and the EU Blue Card, which the residence
authorities then issue. Cases are handled in the AMS regional offices and, for skilled-worker cards, in the
"Fachzentren für die Beschäftigung von Ausländer_innen" (AFZ).

- https://www.ams.at/organisation/kontaktmoeglichkeiten/informationsfreiheit-ams - the AMS's publications under
  the Freedom of Information Act (IFG, in force since 1 September 2025): 126 PDFs, of which about 35 federal
  guidelines (Bundesrichtlinien, "001_brl_..."), the rest contracts and IT orders. No guideline on the AuslBG,
  the Red-White-Red Card or the EU Blue Card is among them. The two "core process" guidelines are the only ones
  that deal with third-country nationals; both are saved.

Temporary-work agencies and the EU Blue Card in these texts
- BRL Kernprozess Arbeitskräfte unterstützen (gültig ab 1.7.2025), section 7.3.3 "Matching und Vermittlung
  durchführen", "Zusätzliche Qualitätsstandards für besondere Personengruppen - Ausländer_innen" (p. 124):
  "Ausländer_innen ohne freien Arbeitsmarktzugang dürfen nicht zu Arbeitskräfteüberlassern vermittelt werden,
  da in diesen Fällen keine amtswegige Beschäftigungsbewilligung erteilt werden kann (§ 4 Abs. 1 Z 7 AuslBG).
  Handelt es sich um Ausländer_innen mit freiem Arbeitsmarktzugang, steht der Vermittlung zu
  Arbeitskräftüberlassern nichts im Wege."
- Neither guideline mentions the EU Blue Card or the Red-White-Red Card, payroll companies or employers of record.

How much real guidance
Little. The AMS publishes its federal guidelines, but none is about the AuslBG, the Red-White-Red Card or the EU
Blue Card; the instructions its AFZ staff apply to those cases are not public. What is public touches the
subject only through the employer-service and jobseeker-service process guidelines saved here.
